Privacy Policy

Power-Clock Alarm · Last updated: October 7, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Developer ("we", "us") handles information when you use the Power-Clock Alarm Android app ("the app"). The app lets you set battery-percentage alarms and time alarms.

In short: we do not require an account, we do not run our own servers, and we do not collect your name, email, phone number or location. Your alarms stay on your device.

2. Information stored on your device

The following stays on your device and is never uploaded by us:

  • Alarm settings: the alarms you create (battery percentages, times, modes, repeat options), your app settings and theme.
  • Audio you choose: if you pick a custom alarm sound with the system file picker, a copy is kept in the app's private storage so the alarm can play it. We do not read any other files.
  • VIP status: if you purchase VIP, a small token confirming your purchase is stored on the device, encrypted with the Android Keystore.

3. Third-party services

Google AdMob (advertising – free version only)

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and your interactions with ads, to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Learn more: How Google uses data when you use our partners' apps and Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we use Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before ads are requested. You can review or change your choice at any time from the app's menu (Privacy settings), when it is shown. You can also reset or disable your advertising ID in your Android settings.

If you have an active VIP purchase, the app does not initialise the ads service or show ads.

Google Play Billing (VIP purchases)

Payments for VIP are handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your card or payment details. The app only receives the status of your purchase (for example, that a subscription or lifetime purchase is active) so it can unlock VIP features. Manage or cancel subscriptions in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.

Report & Feedback

If you use Report & Feedback, the app opens your email app with a pre-filled message. Nothing is sent unless you press send in your email app. The message contains the problem type and the description you wrote, and, if you leave the option ticked, technical details such as app version, Android version, device model, language, battery-optimisation status and whether VIP is active. We use this only to understand and fix the issue you report.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers described above (Google AdMob and Google Play), which process it under their own privacy policies.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your alarms and settings remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app; uninstalling removes all app data stored on the device. Data held by Google (for example, advertising data or purchase records) is governed by Google's policies and your Google Account settings. If you sent us an email, we keep it only as long as needed to respond to you, and you can ask us to delete it.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information the app stores, including encrypting the VIP purchase token with the Android Keystore. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Change or withdraw ads consent from Privacy settings in the app (where available).
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to certain processing. Because we do not collect personal data ourselves, requests about advertising data should be directed to Google; for anything else, contact us below.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page, with the "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
