Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information Battery Alarm Clock (“the app”) handles and your choices. The app is published by Lifestyle Coder (“we”, “us”).

The short version
  • No account, sign-up or login. We do not run a server.
  • Your alarms and settings stay on your device.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. VIP removes them.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play — we never see your payment details.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect, store or sell any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or contacts
  • Your location or the places you visit
  • Your photos, files, messages or call history
  • Details of the alarms you set (they never leave your phone)

Information stored on your device

The app keeps the following only on your device:

  • Your time alarms and battery-level alarms, and their options
  • Your ringtone, vibration, theme and display choices
  • Your VIP status. It is stored encrypted using the Android Keystore and protected against tampering.

Uninstalling the app removes this data from your device.

Ads and your consent choices

The free version uses Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and use information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

Consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions where the law requires it, the app shows Google’s consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before any ads are requested. You can review or change your choice any time from the app menu → Ad privacy settings (shown in regions where it applies).

Other controls. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Buying VIP removes ads from the app.

Purchases (VIP)

VIP plans (monthly, yearly and lifetime) are bought through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy.

The app receives only the purchase information needed to unlock VIP — such as the product and a signed purchase receipt — and checks it on your device. When you are online, the app periodically re-checks your purchase with Google Play so that VIP stays correct after renewals, cancellations or refunds. We never receive your card or payment details.

Reporting a problem or sending feedback

If you use Report a problem / Feedback, the app opens your email app with a message addressed to us. We receive only what you send. If you tick Include device & app info, the email also contains the app version, Android version, device model and the status of alarm-related permissions. It contains no name, contact list or location. You can edit or delete anything before sending. We use it only to understand and fix the problem.

Children

The app is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security and retention

Your data stays on your device. VIP status is encrypted and tamper-protected. Because we do not hold your data on a server, there is nothing for us to retain, export or delete on our side, apart from emails you send us, which we keep only as long as needed to respond.

Your choices and rights

  • Ads: change consent in Ad privacy settings or reset your advertising ID in device settings.
  • Your data: clear the app’s data or uninstall the app to remove everything stored on your device.
  • Requests: if you believe we hold information about you (for example, an email you sent), contact us and we will help you access or delete it, as the law of your region requires.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available inside the app (menu → Privacy Policy) and on this page, with the “Last updated” date at the top.
